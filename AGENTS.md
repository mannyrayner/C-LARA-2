# C-LARA-2 project-aware Codex workflow

For every **substantive** task, follow:

> **orient globally → perform local task → reflect globally**

1. **Orient:** inspect `docs/global_workspace/README.md`,
   `docs/global_workspace/project-intentions.md`, and, when present,
   `docs/global_workspace/current_state.md` sufficiently to understand relevant goals, commitments,
   urgency, successes, concerns, risks, blockers, conflicts, and requests for human input. Use
   judgment; trivial operations do not require mechanical rereading.
2. **Perform:** do the requested work normally, but surface any material conflict with a goal,
   commitment, constraint, or substantially more urgent work. Humans retain prioritization and
   authorization authority.
3. **Reflect:** decide whether the result materially changes the project-level assessment—for
   example by changing evidence about progress, tests, blockers, dependencies, deadlines, risks,
   confidence, conflicts, resolved concerns, or need for human intervention. Completion alone is not
   a reason to change global state.

During the initial review stage, **do not silently edit approved global-workspace state**. If a
material change is indicated, propose it through the reviewed mechanism described in
`docs/global_workspace/README.md`; Manny is the authorizer. ChatGPT C-LARA-Instance may advise but
cannot authorize mutation.

Ordinary per-task reflection complements, and does not replace, the explicit global-review operation.
