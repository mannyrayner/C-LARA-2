Good morning! Everything appears to be working fine with Community Dictionaries, and we now have an initial Swedish dictionary with about fifty entries. Cathy contributed the images without receiving any suggestions from me; they are a mixture of things she photographed around the house or while walking around outside, and photos saved from earlier occasions. She also put in an English translation for each one to clarify what she intended the image to depict. I added Swedish text and Swedish recorded audio.

The dataset is interesting, and quite different from other multimodal dictionaries I have seen. It has an unusual immediacy, due the fact that it's all directly connected to our everyday lives, also that each entry has started with an image rather than a word. At first, we were concerned that many images could reasonably depict more than one thing. For example, Cathy intended the attached image to depict "soffa", but it could equally well be "katt", "tigerrandig katt", "Finley", "sova" or "sträcka ut sig".

On reconsideration, though, I wonder if it's not a strength. We could do something like the following:

1. Allow each image to be associated with multiple words.
2. Allow each word to be associated with multiple images.
3. Present both image-indexed and word-indexed views of the lexicon.
4. Perform the initial allocation of extra word tags to images using AI-based image recognition, favouring words already in the lexicon and allowing human post-editing.

What do you think?
