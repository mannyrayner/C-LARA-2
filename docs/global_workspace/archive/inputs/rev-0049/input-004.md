I have been thinking more about this and also annotating new images that Cathy added to the Swedish dictionary. My strong feeling is that the workflow is still too slow and unintuitive, and also insufficiently image-centred. For AI-supported languages, I'm wondering if we could extend your suggestions above to something like the following:

1. User inputs an image, either though the phone camera or through uploading. Default will be the previous modality.
2. User inputs a sentence describing the image, either in the text language or in the commenting language, and either in voice or in text mode. Defaults carry over here too.
3. AI is used to interpret the combination of image and sentence, trying to reconcile them as well as possible and produce versions in both languages.
4. The user is given feedback in the language and modality they used in step 2, asking for confirmation.
5. If they confirm, all relevant information is added to the dictionary, in particular word entries with text and audio + cross-links.
6. People can flag suspected errors for attention by a dictionary member with suitable linguistic knowledge, but information does not have to be confirmed by the experts to be posted.

How does this sound to you?
