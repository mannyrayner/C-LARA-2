What I think makes most sense here is to create a simple account, readily accessible to a broad audience and disseminated quickly, with the short- to medium-term goal of encouraging other groups to try using Community Dictionaries. Then we will be able to collect nontrivial user data and understand better how we can further improve the platform. At the metalevel, we may also be able to improve our understanding of how projects of this general kind can be organised.



Here are some more detailed thoughts:



1. Short, non-technical paper.&#x20;
2. Post on AiraXiv and ResearchGate, both of which allow AI authors and quick posting.&#x20;
3. I will also post a review on my Goodreads account, which is widely read.
4. Stress that the platform can easily be used now by people with zero technical expertise. Browsing existing content is just pointing and clicking, and incurs no costs. The platform works well on both phones (primarily suitable for browsing and adding entries) and laptops (primarily useful for editing). Include enough screenshots to show how easy it all is.
5. Creating a new dictionary requires zero expertise.&#x20;
6. Adding new images is just taking pictures through a camera (or uploading them) and saving. Zero expertise and free.
7. Manually adding words, translations/explanations and recordings again requires zero expertise and is free, though somewhat laborious. This is what one does for a non-AI-supported language..&#x20;
8. Creating the sentence and word pages for an AI-enabled language takes two or three clicks from the Settings tab, and only the dictionary owner needs to know how to do it. The result should ideally be reviewed by a fluent speaker of the text language, but the first cut is typically good (over 95%).
9. We can create simple exercises (flashcards, crosswords, word scrambles) in both kinds of languages, for low but not zero cost.
10. We will make available some money to support groups who want to experiment. We could say that we offer USD 10 initially, then more if they use it up to a maximum of (say) USD 50. We require that they share the dictionary with us, so that we can examine it, but promise not to share specific content with third parties. We do however reserve the right to share overall statistics, like the number of entries.
11. At the metalevel, stress that the project is no way finished. Indeed the most interesting thing about it is that putting a strong AI in the centre means that we have been able to develop things so quickly, going through multiple design iterations in just a couple of weeks while still keeping things simple and usable.&#x20;
12. What we most want to discover from (10) is whether we can continue to improve the design in the same way by quickly responding to user feedback.
