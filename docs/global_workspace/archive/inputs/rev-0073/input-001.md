This is an excellent start and I think already close to something we could post. Some suggestions:

1. Clarify where the initial ideas came from. The immediate predecessor was C-LARA-2. We should cite the ResearchGate paper, [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/410729838_An_AI-Based_Platform_That_Can_Explain_Its_Own_Functioning_Why_Your_Project_Should_Be_Fully_Codex-Generated,](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/410729838_An_AI-Based_Platform_That_Can_Explain_Its_Own_Functioning_Why_Your_Project_Should_Be_Fully_Codex-Generated,) as well as the repo. We should also mention that C-LARA-2 was in turn based on C-LARA and cite e.g. [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398798681_AI-Generated_Tailor-Made_Pedagogical_Picture_Books_How_Close_Are_We](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398798681_AI-Generated_Tailor-Made_Pedagogical_Picture_Books_How_Close_Are_We). Feedback from the C-LARA/C-LARA-2 user group, in particular Sophie Rendina and Branislav Bédi, stressed the importance of making the app simpler and mobile-friendly (both Rendina and Bédi) and more suitable for Indigenous communities (Rendina).
2. C-LARA-2 was developed using various versions of ChatGPT-5 and Codex. Experimentation with the newly available ChatGPT-6 Astra Extra High, and widespread positive coverage in the media, immediately suggested that a much faster development cycle should be possible with this more powerful model. Part of our goal was to determine whether this was indeed true, by attempting a complete rewrite of the platform. Our experiences strongly suggest that our hopes were realistic. It is generally possible to suggest a major new feature, or a major rewrite of an existing feature, and get a working, installable patch from Astra in less than an hour.
3. Mention that, in accordance with our general plan of having a development cycle that is accelerated as much as possible using AI means, we are posting this paper on the new AiraXiv server, which offers much faster turnaround than conventional venues.
