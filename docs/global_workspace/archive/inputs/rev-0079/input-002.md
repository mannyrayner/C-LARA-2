I'm thinking that the next thing to do is perhaps to set up basic infrastructure to support evaluation, so that we can get some figures for the initial dictionaries. Perhaps the evaluation tab first asks them some questions about their relevant language competences (alternately, this could go in Profile). If they have appropriate competences, they can evaluate the different aspects of the dictionary, e.g. "match of sentence to image", "correctness of sentence audio", "match of sentence to translation", "correctness of extracted words for a sentence", "correctness of word translations", "correctness of word audio".

We would also want functionality to summarise the results, and, if we have more than one evaluator for a set, get inter-rater statistics.

An intriguing question to investigate is the idea of AI-based evaluation. Perhaps a more powerful model can evaluate the output of a less powerful one, or you can repeat an evaluation several times and use majority voting, or something along those lines?
