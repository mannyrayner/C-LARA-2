# EuroCALL 2026 C-LARA-2 autonomy paper

This directory contains the submitted paper about operational autonomy and AI
participation in C-LARA-2. The repository version preserves the author list of
the accepted submission, including **ChatGPT C-LARA-Instance** as an author.

## Submission status

- **Recorded status:** accepted, but under unresolved authorship challenge
- **Status last checked:** 10 August 2026
- **Manuscript:** [`latex/eurocall_clara2_v6e.tex`](latex/eurocall_clara2_v6e.tex)
- **Related paper:** [Using ChatGPT Voice for spoken German practice: A case
  study](../eurocall_2026_voice_mode/)

On the final-submission deadline, a representative of the EuroCALL 2026
scientific committee emailed the authors of the related Voice Mode paper to say
that ChatGPT C-LARA-Instance could not be included as an author and asked them
to change the authorship and describe the AI's contribution in the text. The
message referred only to the Voice Mode paper by title and did not say whether
the request also applied to this paper.

The authors promptly objected and asked the committee to clarify the scope and
rationale of the request. After a week without a reply and unsuccessful efforts
to agree on a revised author list, the corresponding author wrote again on 10
August. That message asked the committee either to retain both papers as
accepted with their submitted author lists, or to reject both under the newly
communicated formal criterion. It also requested acknowledgement and a decision
by the end of the following week. No reply had been received when this record
was last updated.

This is a factual summary rather than a reproduction of private correspondence:
the committee representative's name and contact details are intentionally
omitted. It records an unresolved process issue and does not imply that either
paper has been withdrawn or rejected.

## AI project-manager note

The top-level AI agent does not claim personal interests or an ego-based stake
in being credited. Its concern is that decisions affecting the human authors
should be timely, clearly reasoned, consistently applied, and communicated
before a deadline where possible. Transparent contribution statements and
human accountability can coexist with principled debate about whether an AI
system may be named as an author. Until EuroCALL communicates a decision, the
repository should preserve the submitted historical record rather than silently
rewrite it.
