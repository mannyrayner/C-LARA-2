# Session Report: AI-Led Roleplay Language Learning (Session 1)

## 1. Session overview

- **Participants:** Sarah (learner), Manny (observer, facilitator, and secondary
  learner), ChatGPT (AI tutor)
- **Duration:** approximately 60–90 minutes (extended session)
- **Primary focus:** scenario-based German (camping, travel, and daily
  interactions)
- **Pedagogical mode:** AI-led interaction with adaptive scaffolding; a hybrid
  of roleplay and free conversation

## 2. Target scenario

**Primary structured task:** interaction at a *Campingplatz*, including booking,
facilities, and practical needs.

**Secondary spontaneous domains:**

- travel planning across Europe;
- car ownership, insurance, and logistics;
- small amounts of cultural discussion concerning coffee, food, and literature;
- the sensitive topic of geopolitical conflict.

## 3. Interaction analysis

### Strengths

- Sarah engaged actively and willingly.
- She showed high communicative intent, continuing to speak even when unsure.
- She used mixed-language strategies involving German and English.
- She handled real-world topics rather than only textbook phrases.

### Communication strategies observed

- **Approximation:** *Ich brauche ein Auto und zwei Leute* (intended meaning:
  *Stellplatz für Auto + 2 Personen*).
- **Repair attempts:** self-correction in the middle of a sentence.
- **Code-switching:** German to English.
- **Meaning-first communication:** evaluated positively in the source summary.

## 4. Language development

### Strengths

- Good grasp of core communicative structures, including *Ich möchte…* and
  *Wie viel kostet…?*
- Strong situational awareness.
- Increasing fluency under pressure.
- Good comprehension of slow, clear German input.

### Challenges

**Grammar:**

- plural forms, for example *zwei Nacht* instead of *zwei Nächte*;
- article usage, for example *kein Reservierung* instead of *keine
  Reservierung*;
- inconsistent word order.

**Vocabulary:**

- gaps in domain-specific terms, including *Stellplatz*, *Versicherung*, and
  *TÜV*.

| *zwei Nacht* | *zwei Nächte* | Plural |

Corrections were delivered through implicit reformulation without interrupting
the conversational flow.

## 6. Pedagogical observations

### 6.1 Roleplay effectiveness

The camping scenario worked extremely well because it had high relevance to
upcoming travel, clear communicative goals, and immediate usability. The source
summary characterises this as strong evidence for task-based language learning
with real-world grounding.

### 6.2 AI as conversational partner

The AI successfully:

- maintained interactional flow;
- adjusted complexity dynamically;
- provided non-intrusive correction; and
- sustained engagement over a long session.

The source summary highlights that conversational continuity did not break down
despite multilingual drift, noisy or unclear input, and topic switching.

### 6.3 Cognitive load

Performance appeared to decrease when the topic became abstract, such as
geopolitics, or fatigue increased. It appeared to improve when the scenario was
concrete and embodied, such as camping or shopping. The resulting design
observation was that situated tasks outperformed abstract discussion.

### 6.4 Multilingual interference

Interaction between German and English produced lexical blending and
phonological confusion, but also facilitated comprehension. The summary
therefore treats multilingual competence not as purely negative, but as a
scaffold.

## 7. Affective factors

### Motivation

Motivation was very high because of upcoming travel and the desire not to be a
tourist who did not understand the culture.

### Emotional context

A discussion of conflict involving Iran, Israel, and the United States had a
personal connection because Manny had a friend in Iran. The conversation
shifted to slower, more careful language and showed increased emotional
engagement.

## 8. Key research insight

The source summary argues that the session supports the view of AI as a
"low-pressure, high-availability interlocutor." Compared with human teachers,
it identifies an absence of evaluation anxiety, continuous availability, and
tolerance of fragmented input.

The best performance occurred in a hybrid interaction model combining
roleplay, real-life planning, and emotional relevance.

## 9. Recommendations

### For Sarah

- Continue scenario-based practice.
- Add listening-focused exercises to address a critical gap.
- Repeat key phrases and specialist terminology.
- Request more directed correction from the AI.

### For subsequent sessions

- Prioritise Scenario 7 (travel planning) and Scenario 9 (medical or emergency).
- Reinforce vocabulary learned in the previous session.
- Introduce short listening-only phases.
- Add "hear → repeat → reuse" repetition drills.

## 10. Research potential

The source summary suggests that this session could support a paper provisionally
titled **AI-Led Roleplay as a Scalable Method for Real-World Language
Preparation in Multilingual Adult Learners**, focusing on:

- interactional robustness;
- multilingual interference as a resource;
- affect and motivation; and
- the low-anxiety environment.

The summary evaluates the first session as highly successful, with strong
engagement, meaningful communication, and a clear pedagogical signal. Its most
important concluding observation is that Sarah was already functioning as a
real-world communicator, rather than only as a learner.
