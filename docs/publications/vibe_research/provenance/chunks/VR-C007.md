[Version 2]

A couple of things now seem especially promising to me.

First, C-LARA-2 and How Woke is Grok? make a remarkably good contrasting pair. The C-LARA-2 description on the site explicitly says the platform was completely rewritten with all the implementation work performed by Codex, so it gives us the “months of sustained collaboration” end of the spectrum. How Woke is Grok?, meanwhile, really was a compact empirical study, comparing Grok-4 with GPT-5, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek using ten polarising statements. The contrast lets us make a much more interesting claim than “AI makes research faster”: lower transaction costs alter which research questions it is rational to pursue at all.

Second, I think we may have found the conceptual spine of the paper:

vibe coding → asymmetry → accountability → provenance → authorship → incentives for disclosure → prevalence as an empirical question.

That sequence feels much less confrontational than beginning with authorship, while still arriving at the authorship issue by a fairly inexorable route.
