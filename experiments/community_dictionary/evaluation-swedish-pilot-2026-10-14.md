## Error interpretation

Manny clarifies that the major extraction problem was a couple of missing words.
He describes nearly all word-audio errors as tone errors, with one consonant error:
*person* sounded approximately like *perthon*. These descriptions come from Manny,
not independent audio analysis. The eight non-acceptable word-audio judgements occur
in seven sampled sentence contexts, all with acceptable sentence-audio judgements.
Different surface forms, prompts, voices, generation dates and whole-sentence versus
word-level listening could affect comparisons; this is not yet a controlled TTS test.

The first CSV export lacked readable entry/word texts. Its IDs and fingerprints
cannot by themselves identify the individual words outside the database. This
motivates the export follow-up, rather than attempting to infer the words from IDs.
No raw CSV, private media or per-participant research dataset is committed here.
