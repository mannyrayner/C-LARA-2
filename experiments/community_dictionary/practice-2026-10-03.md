## Delivered scope

One Practise tab; optional category selection; six self-assessed flashcard modes;
interactive picture crosswords; and picture word searches, retaining C-LARA's
existing meaning of "word scramble". No schema change, new dependency, stored
exercise copies, learner history or automatic content generation. Practice is
available to participating ordinary members as well as editors.

The grid builders were extracted unchanged from the C-LARA project view module
into a pure shared module; an AST comparison verifies that extraction. The new
adapter uses accepted live contributions, linked pictures and current audio.
Membership and accepted status are checked at page/data/media boundaries.
Open-game content is revalidated before actions, every 30 visible seconds and
on returning to the page. Withdrawal and edits invalidate games; failed
connectivity clears the activity. Already delivered content cannot be recalled.
