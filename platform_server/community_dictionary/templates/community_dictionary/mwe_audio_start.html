This optional pilot uses short spoken carrier phrases, then extracts the expression between two pauses. It supports French, Swedish, German, Italian and Icelandic multi-word entries, plus explicitly selected Japanese word entries. Boundary checks cannot guarantee correct pronunciation; please listen afterwards.

{% else %}

Multi-word entries are identified by spaces. Single-word compounds and unspaced separable-verb lemmas are kept. German, Italian and Icelandic carriers are new and need listening tests.

{% endif %}

Only accepted word entries with existing AI audio are eligible. Human recordings, sentences and successful replacements from this recipe are kept. Each shared word page is processed once. New audio replaces the old only after extraction succeeds; earlier recordings are retained and can be restored.

New recordings use the voice of the latest existing AI recording (Marin if its voice is unavailable). Up to five completed recordings may be tried per expression. Network errors and timeouts are not automatically retried.

For a comparison: first finish a word-audio evaluation under Evaluate. After regeneration, use “Repeat these word-audio items” on that closed evaluation set.
